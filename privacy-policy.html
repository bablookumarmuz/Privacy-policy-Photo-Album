Privacy Policy for Android Photo Album

Last updated: September 30, 2025

1. Information We Collect

  • Photos and media stored locally on your device (we do not upload them to any server).
  • Basic app usage data (to improve performance).
  • No personally identifiable information is collected unless you choose to share it.

2. How We Use Information

  • To allow you to create, manage, and view photo albums.
  • To improve app stability and performance.

3. Data Storage & Sharing

Your photos remain on your device. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. If cloud backup options are enabled in the future, you will be asked for explicit consent.

4. Security

The app does not transmit your private photos over the internet. All albums remain in your device storage.

5. Children’s Privacy

This app is not directed towards children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

6. Your Rights

  • You may delete the app at any time to remove all stored data.
  • Since we do not store your data on servers, deletion is immediate upon uninstall.
